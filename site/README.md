# eventhorizon on meow-meow.io

The page lives in the site repository at
`~/data/programming/meow-meow.io/eventhorizon/`, with its recordings in
`media/`. These two pieces go into that repository's shared files, and are kept
here until nothing else is editing them:

- `tile.html` goes in `index.html`, inside "Works you can open" (the
  `<div class="works">` list), wherever it should sit among the other works.
- `updates-entry.json` goes at the top of the array in `data/updates.json`.

The recordings were made with `tools/capture.py`; the commands are in
`site/media-jobs.txt`. The tile's dots are sampled from `media/plate.webp`.
